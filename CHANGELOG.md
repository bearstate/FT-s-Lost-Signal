# FT's Geology: Lost Signal 0.1.0

The first release, a beta. Forge 1.20.1 (47.2 or newer), with FT's Geology 0.11.4, Feel the Nature 1.2.0, Simple Voice
Chat 2.6 and the Simple Radio ham fork.

Every line is voiced: the District, the lost hikers (their breath and calls too), the keepers, the man on the radio who
is not who he says, and the stranger, by day and through the door at night. Subtitles show the words as well.

- **Lookouts with people in them**: empty, kept, abandoned in a hurry, or kept by the stranger; changing from dawn to
  dawn.
- **Keepers**: a name, a voice, a temper; talk to them, run their errands, earn their trust, stay the night. When he
  comes to the door a nervous keeper gets under the bed; tell any of them to hide. One still standing when the door
  gives is taken, and in the morning the lookout is one left in a hurry.
- **The rescue frequency**: the District's day, calls to and from the lookouts round you, the rescue panel (`J`).
  The District's own calls are answered from the panel: the radio check, the end of day check, and the weather, sent
  as the cab's instruments read it (FT's Geology's weather terminal); unanswered, it asks once more. What comes over
  the air is shown only where it reaches your set, by the radio's own propagation: behind the hills a voice is static,
  or nothing; the panel lists the lookouts round with their S-meter readings, the ones out of reach last.
- **Lost hikers**: guided in by radio, by the fire finder's bearings, by flares, smoke and the signal lamp. Found,
  they are talked to face to face: told to follow, to wait, or the way to the lookout; they say so when they cannot
  follow where you went. Come in, they go up the stair and in at the cab's door for the night, and down again in the
  morning. A lamp waved at night is a bright swinging light seen from far off, the lantern in their hand; by day
  they say you would never see it. The night's monsters hunt them as they hunt villagers and they run from them; one
  killed is lost, the scream heard on the mountain, what is left of them where it happened (hikers.monsters).
- **One thing at a time**: no one says a line over their own last one; the stranger is only ever in one place.
- **Older lookouts made safe**: one built before Feel the Nature brought its stair out onto the walkway gets a hatch
  over the stairwell in its floor, and lanterns under it.
- **Forest fires** in forest country, seen far off: smoke from a few places across it, dark off the burning and
  greying as it rises, leaning and drifting on FT's Geology's wind, an orange glow under it at night, flames close to
  that burn nothing. Read off the fire finder, reported and crossed. Fire really burning round a lookout (lightning,
  a campfire got away, another mod's wildfires) is found too, and the crew sent to it puts it out.
- **Supply crates** at the foot of the trail.
- **The stranger**: by day a man with things to say; by night the mask, the door, the window. He comes up to the
  lookout on foot: up the trail, up the stair, along the walkway, seen from the windows if you look (and he looks back).
  Masked, he can be fought, and does not run; driven off, he does not fall but goes back into the dark, and comes again
  sooner and worse. Hiding under the bed, looking out from under it at the room.
- **The radio used against you**: calls from no one, and a player's own voice played back.
- **What is left** where someone was taken, and their journal: each written its own way, with a page for their last
  night (the weather, where they said they were, the last thing the lookout told them, the whistling if they heard it).
- **The flowing sleep**.
- **A world's settings** for every fright, in `serverconfig/fts_lostsignal-server.toml`.
- English and Turkish.
- Tried on a dedicated server with a client joining: the keepers, the panel, the calls, the hikers, the visits and
  hiding work across the two.
